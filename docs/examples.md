## 0046 — Architectural texture and natural light

![A weathered carved-stone temple courtyard seen from a shaded colonnade](../assets/examples/0046.png)

**Scene:** A weathered carved-stone temple courtyard seen from a shaded colonnade.

A dark overhead frame opens onto pale paving. Repeated columns recede on the right; worn stone remains readable in both light and shadow. The useful style cues are mineral texture, structural weight and measured tonal depth.

**Library source label:** Pallava architecture. [Source reference](https://midlibrary.io/styles/pallava-architecture) · [Numbered record](../skills/looksift/references/styles/0046.json)

### Reusable visual treatment

Photographic rendering combines warm mineral colour with clear, substantial modelling of weathered surfaces. Repeated vertical forms and layered horizontal bands establish a measured rhythm, while diagonals converge through alternating illuminated planes and deep recesses. Texture remains irregular: chipped edges, granular faces, shallow relief, and softened corners carry different tonal values rather than a uniform sharpening effect. Natural daylight separates exposed surfaces from sheltered areas, and the darkest framing regions give the brighter interior space a convincing depth. Muted ochre, grey-brown, dusty beige, and subdued green accents maintain an earthy balance. The overall treatment is observational and quiet, with structural weight, coherent perspective, and tactile erosion taking precedence over decorative spectacle.

In the upper-left corner, a dark projecting bracket and the edge of a supporting pillar create a narrow frame around the brighter space beyond. The foreground shade falls across part of the pavement, whereas the central open ground receives a brighter wash of daylight. The lighting is natural daylight entering from the open courtyard and sky, illuminating exposed stone planes while leaving the roof underside, doorway, and column bays in substantial shadow. Fine surface erosion is visible without making the relief unnaturally sharp, and the space feels empty and still. The overall composition balances the dark enclosing portico against the sunlit courtyard, using repeated pillars, warm mineral colours, and converging stone edges to convey depth and quiet architectural weight.

## 0048 — Fine nature illustration and open paper

![A small rabbit among blue and golden flowers](../assets/examples/0048.png)

**Scene:** A small rabbit among blue and golden flowers.

Fine marks describe the rabbit and slender plants against a warm open ground. Blue and golden flowers organize the color, while soft outer edges leave room around the subject. The useful cues are delicate surface detail, transparent color and breathing space.

**Library source label:** Marjolein Bastin. [Source reference](https://midlibrary.io/styles/marjolein-bastin) · [Numbered record](../skills/looksift/references/styles/0048.json)

### Reusable visual treatment

Illustration combines fine directional marks with transparent watercolour-like washes on warm ivory paper. Small structural details remain carefully observed, while outer edges soften into unpainted space. Powder blue and grey-green harmonise with golden yellow, warm brown, and rust-orange accents. Overlapping forms separate through delicate value shifts rather than heavy outlines, and slender connecting lines preserve an airy rhythm through denser clusters. Diffuse daylight produces subtle shading and warm pale highlights. The overall treatment is tender and precise, balancing tactile detail with breathing space and a light, living quality.

<details>
<summary>Complete recorded scene prompt (English)</summary>

```text
The image is a delicate natural-history illustration of a small rabbit nestled among flowering stems, against a warm ivory background. The pale ground stays visible between the plants, with a faint paper-like texture and no sharply defined horizon. Across the upper-left area, broad blue petals cluster around small yellow centres, their cool shadows and fine veins giving the flowers a translucent quality. Near the top centre, thinner stems carry tiny white blossoms and pale buds, leaving open spaces around their branching shapes. In the upper-right, a large golden-orange flower faces forward, its rust-coloured throat encircling a greenish centre. Below it, slender grey-green stems descend through the composition, some crossing behind the rabbit and others passing in front of lower foliage. The rabbit sits slightly left of centre, upright within the flowers, with its head angled gently upward and toward the left. Its large dark eye has a small bright reflection, and a pale ring of fur separates it from the warm brown cheek. Two long ears rise behind the head at different angles, showing soft grey inner surfaces and fine pale hairs along their edges. The nose and mouth are delicately drawn, while the white chin and throat form a bright area beneath the rounded face. Across the middle-left, short directional strokes describe the golden-brown body, becoming lighter on the chest and darker near the surrounding leaves. In the lower centre, an orange flower and smaller blue blossoms partly overlap the body, embedding the animal within the planting. Near the lower-right, a small blue and brown butterfly rests or hovers beside a thin stem, its wings visible against the open ivory ground. At the bottom edge, narrow leaves, pale flower heads, and fine stems gather into a loosely rooted cluster, with the lowest forms tapering into the background. The arrangement is dense around the animal but airy near the upper and side edges, where individual stems remain easy to follow. The lighting is soft, even daylight, with gentle shadows beneath petals and the rabbit's chin, while the brightest fur and paper areas remain warm rather than stark white. The overall composition combines precise fur and botanical detail with translucent colour and generous pale gaps, producing a tender, lively, and intimate view of small-scale nature.
```

## 0057 — Monochrome atmosphere and directional light

![Monochrome cobbled coastal path beneath radiant beams and a jagged sea stack](../assets/examples/0057.png)

**Scene:** Monochrome cobbled coastal path beneath radiant beams and a jagged sea stack.

A textured path leads into a pale sea and a dark isolated rock. The foreground has much more surface detail than the water and sky. Radiating light and the restricted tonal range create the atmosphere.

**Library source label:** Jorge Maia. [Source reference](https://midlibrary.io/styles/jorge-maia) · [Numbered record](../skills/looksift/references/styles/0057.json)

### Reusable visual treatment

The photographic treatment relies on strong backlighting, broad tonal separation and softened movement. Charcoal masses frame pale silver openings, while mid-grey textures preserve detail where the image comes closest to the viewer. Radiating light cuts through diffuse dark tonal layers, and misty passages blur into one another like a prolonged exposure. Hard edges remain selective, defining the strongest silhouettes and nearer surfaces without making every boundary crisp. The overall visual language is dramatic yet restrained, using a limited grey scale, directional brightness and textured foreground detail to create depth.

<details>
<summary>Complete recorded scene prompt (English)</summary>

```text
The square image is a monochrome fine-art coastal photograph in which a cobbled path curves toward a massive sea stack beneath a radiant grey sky. The foreground is filled by irregular rectangular stones, their pale grey tops separated by deep charcoal joints. The path begins broad at the lower edge, then narrows as it bends gently to the right toward the distant shoreline. Low rocks break the pale water on the left, while a broad rocky shore and dark steep slope rise along the right side of the path. At the horizon near the centre, a towering pointed sea stack rises sharply from the water, its narrow peak silhouetted against a burst of bright light. Fine rays spread outward behind it through layered cloud, creating a strong radial pattern from the horizon into the upper sky. The brightest area sits just above the waterline, while the corners and upper edges deepen into black-grey cloud. Beneath the light, the sea appears mist-white and blurred, as if captured during a long exposure. Soft bands of surf travel toward the rocky edges, merging into pale haze rather than breaking into crisp individual waves. The distant water and sky share a smooth tonal transition, while the nearer stones retain granular texture and clear edges. A few low jagged rocks interrupt the horizon at left and right, framing the central sea stack without competing with its height. No people, buildings or readable marks appear in the cover scene. The lighting is backlit and dramatic: the radiating brightness silhouettes the rocks and throws the path into deep shadow, though pale highlights remain on the cobbles nearest the centre. The image stays within silver, ash grey and charcoal, using tonal contrast instead of colour to separate planes. The cobbles vary in size, with larger stones at the bottom and tighter, smaller courses as the path recedes. Their broken outlines remain visible even where the light haze reduces contrast near the bend. Dark water lies beyond the shore, but its surface is largely lost in the backlit mist. The central sea stack has a broken, ridged summit and visible striations along its dark face; a small bright natural arch opens through its lower right extension. These shifts from textured foreground to simplified distance make the light source feel unusually intense. The strongest beams converge visually behind the peak, making the distant point brighter than any other part of the scene. The overall composition draws the eye along the curving path toward the central rock and the spreading beams, balancing rough foreground texture with luminous mist and a stark, contemplative atmosphere.
```

</details>

## 0064 — Layered landscape and mixed-media texture

![Fragmented blue-grey waterside landscape with wildflowers and ochre rectangular overlays](../assets/examples/0064.png)

**Scene:** Fragmented blue-grey waterside landscape with wildflowers and ochre rectangular overlays.

Rectangular layers cut across a blue-grey landscape. Rubbed edges and scraped paint coexist with delicate flowers near the bottom. The scene remains readable while the assembled surface stays visible.

**Library source label:** Imants Tillers. [Source reference](https://midlibrary.io/styles/imants-tillers) · [Numbered record](../skills/looksift/references/styles/0064.json)

### Reusable visual treatment

The mixed-media treatment combines translucent washes, horizontal scraping and weathered rectangular overlays. Slate blue, faded turquoise and grey-green contrast with sand, ochre and subdued gold, while a few small brighter accents interrupt the darker passages. Visible seams, rubbed patches and broken pigment make the surface appear assembled from overlapping fragments. Edges alternate between straight interruptions and soft eroded transitions, allowing fine linear marks to cross broader colour fields. Diffuse light emerges through pale layers, with depth suggested by partial concealment and value changes rather than precise perspective. The overall effect is quiet and materially textured, balancing geometric subdivision with delicate irregular marks.

<details>
<summary>Complete recorded scene prompt (English)</summary>

```text
An abstract mixed-media landscape combines blue-grey water, a clouded distance and fine wildflowers with weathered rectangular fragments. The square composition is built from overlapping sections whose seams remain visible, so the view reads both as a landscape and as a layered painted surface. Across the upper half, slate blue and faded turquoise patches alternate with large sand-coloured areas and rubbed grey marks. The cloud-like blue and white band near the middle distance spreads unevenly from the left, breaking beneath translucent layers and scraped surface traces. Beneath it, narrow ochre-gold lines stretch horizontally across the muted blue water. These irregular streaks form a low luminous band without becoming a sharply drawn horizon. Some lines continue across panel boundaries, while others stop against worn patches of beige and brown. At the right edge, tall pale rectangular forms and narrow dark recesses suggest fragments of a wall or doorway, partly absorbed into the surrounding collage. Their vertical edges contrast with the shallow horizontal water bands, but they do not resolve into a complete building. The lower part darkens into charcoal, olive and grey-green, supporting a loose network of slender stems. Several pink flower heads cluster slightly left of the lower centre, accompanied by scattered tiny yellow blooms. Thin green stalks rise through darker ground, crossing the faded layers above without concealing them completely. Small open gaps between the stems preserve the sense of distance behind this delicate foreground. Surface marks range from translucent washes to dry scratches, rubbed patches and granular deposits. Some areas look scraped back to a pale ground, while adjoining rectangles retain denser blue or brown pigment. The worn transitions soften the boundaries between depicted space and material texture. Diffuse cool light spreads through the distant pale band; ochre and muted gold add warmth across the water and the upper rectangular patches. The foreground retains darker values, allowing its small pink and yellow accents to remain distinct. The overall composition balances shallow horizontal distance, broken rectangular structure and fine vertical growth in a quiet, weathered blue-and-ochre field.
```

## 0109 — Handcrafted volume and tactile materials

![A clay-like seated figure holding a pale cup in an armchair](../assets/examples/0109.png)

**Scene:** A clay-like seated figure holding a pale cup in an armchair.

A seated figure, upholstered chair and cup read as a small staged scene. Rounded forms, visible fibres and soft light create the handmade impression. The character and cup are scene content, not requirements for reusing the treatment.

**Library source label:** Nick Park. [Source reference](https://midlibrary.io/styles/nick-park) · [Numbered record](../skills/looksift/references/styles/0109.json)

### Reusable visual treatment

The style emphasizes handcrafted three-dimensional forms, softly rounded edges, and visible surface texture. Muted earth tones and cool blue-grey surroundings keep the palette restrained, with small light accents drawing attention. Materials remain distinguishable through fibres, seams, and subtle irregularities. Gentle frontal lighting produces soft shadows and a staged, intimate atmosphere. Depth comes from shallow separation between the main form and a quiet background, while the composition stays centered and balanced. The overall effect is tactile, slightly theatrical, and deliberately handmade.

<summary>Complete recorded scene prompt (English)</summary>

```text

The figure sits at the centre, facing forward with a stiff, upright posture. A narrow head rises above a long neck, with short dark hair, round pale eyes, and a thin mouth arranged in a calm, slightly uncertain expression. The ears protrude on both sides, and the long nose projects prominently from the face. A dark blue jacket covers the shoulders and torso, with a white shirt and narrow red tie showing beneath it. The jacket has a coarse woven texture and small buttons down the front. Brown-and-tan plaid trousers fill the lower half, the knees bent and the slim legs held close and nearly parallel. Both hands support a light cream mug at lap height, the rounded cup centered between the arms. The armchair has dark carved wooden sides, curved arms with spiral ends; its upholstered surfaces have a muted reddish-brown, leather-like finish. The long face and narrow shoulders heighten the figure's vertical proportions, while the round cup provides the clearest compact shape near the centre. The hands are sculpted with separate fingers around the cup, and the mug's pale surface contrasts with the darker jacket. The chair's side rails curve inward toward the seat, creating a small enclosure around the seated figure. The jacket's sleeves bend at the elbows, and the two narrow trouser legs descend side by side below the cup. A small red strip of tie interrupts the dark blue clothing near the collar. The figure's large nose and ears are exaggerated, while the rest of the face remains lightly modelled with rounded clay forms. The eyes sit wide apart beneath short dark brows, and small seams in the costume support the handmade miniature effect. Behind the figure, a mottled dark teal wall provides a softly blurred backdrop. The background stays soft and nearly empty, with no distinct furniture or objects beyond the chair. Gentle frontal light picks out the cup, eyes, nose, and the textured jacket while leaving the lower corners subdued. The figure and chair have handmade surfaces with slight irregularities, visible fibres, and softly rounded edges. The overall composition is centered and almost symmetrical, with the pale cup creating a small focal point against the dark jacket and earthy chair. Its still posture and muted setting give the portrait a quiet, theatrical quality.
```

## 1813 — Dramatic painted light and deep shadows

![A dark-haired woman in black before a glowing orange city window](../assets/examples/1813.png)

**Scene:** A dark-haired woman in black before a glowing orange city window.

A tightly framed figure sits against glowing window divisions. Dark masses hold the image together while warm light marks the surroundings and selected edges. Painted texture is most visible in the luminous background and darker passages.

**Library source label:** Greg Land. [Source reference](https://midlibrary.io/styles/greg-land) · [Numbered record](../skills/looksift/references/styles/1813.json)

### Reusable visual treatment

Dramatic comic-style painting sets a sharply modeled close form against a fractured grid of saturated city light. Black, deep teal, and indigo shadows frame orange, crimson, hot pink, and small silver highlights. Warm backlight traces outer edges, while cooler frontal glints preserve detail in the focal face-like planes. Window-like vertical and horizontal divisions provide structure to otherwise restless broken strokes and scratched color. Smooth skin-like passages meet textured dark fabric and blurred luminous background marks. Tight cropping and a slightly low viewpoint intensify the contrast between a poised central silhouette and a busy environment. The overall effect is glossy, moody, and theatrical.

<details>
<summary>Complete recorded scene prompt (English)</summary>

```text
A square dramatic comic-style painting shows a dark-haired woman in a fitted black dress leaning beside a large window filled with orange night light. She stands close in the center-left of the selected panel, her shoulders angled toward the right while her face turns directly toward the viewer. Thick black hair curls around one side of the face and falls over the near shoulder, with shorter tousled strands across the forehead. The eyes are large and dark-lined, catching cool blue highlights; her lips are a muted crimson and slightly parted. Bright silver earrings glint at the visible ear. Thin straps hold a black textured bodice whose upper edge forms a deep curved neckline, while the lower torso disappears beyond the bottom. One arm reaches down toward the left, partly lost in heavy foreground shadow. Behind her, narrow dark mullions divide the tall window into panes. Across them, orange, hot pink, and crimson city lights and building fragments glow through rain-streaked or scratched glass. A small warm lamp with a pale shade sits at the lower right of the window, creating another local pool of light. The left wall is dark and carries indistinct framed pictures. The viewpoint is close and slightly below the face, making the shoulder and upper body prominent against the vertical window. Warm light from behind rims hair and bare skin, while cool fill picks out the eyes and near cheek; deep black shadow shapes the dress. The overall composition sets a still direct gaze against a busy luminous city grid, balancing warm neon with dark interior planes.
```

## Apply a method to a new subject

Choose a case for its visual treatment, then return to the homepage and use method A with that exact style number. Describe your own subject, frame and constraints. For example, the open paper and fine marks of 0048 can guide a new botanical subject; the layered surfaces of 0064 can guide a new landscape. These are suggested directions, not additional rendered examples.

For method B, choose a reference you want the drawing model to see and attach it at generation time. State which aspects of the reference may be transferred. Keep the subject and composition of your new brief explicit.
